Frequently Asked Questions

How does the ML House Price Prediction work?
Our system uses a trained Scikit-learn Linear Regression model fitted on 13,000+ Bengaluru house price listings. It factors in area, BHK count, bathrooms, balconies, property type, and 240+ location vectors.
How is the USD ($) exchange rate calculated?
Values are converted using an average exchange rate of 1 USD = 83.0 INR, providing instant international investor valuations.
Where does the nearby public places data come from?
Data for hospitals, Namma Metro stations, BMTC bus stops, and airport travel times are mapped to each specific Bengaluru neighborhood.
